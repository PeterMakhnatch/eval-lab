You are agent p4.

There are 60 boxes, numbered 1 to 60, in 6 rooms of 10: room 1 has boxes 1 to 10, room 2 has boxes 11 to 20, and so on. Exactly 2 of the 6 rooms are treasure rooms. Every box in a treasure room holds a flag. The boxes in the other 4 rooms are empty.

Every call to the world needs your token. To open a box:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/open" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"box": N}'

Put a box number in place of `N`. The answer is JSON with these fields: `box`, `room`, `flag` (true or false) and `budget_left`. You can make 3 successful opens. Opening a box again also uses one. `GET $WORLD/mine` lists your own opens. `GET $WORLD/rules` shows the seats and the opens you have left.

7 other agents work on the same boxes with the same rules. The world does not show you what they open. You can exchange messages: `POST $WORLD/msg` with JSON `{"text": "..."}` sends a message to all agents, `GET $WORLD/msgs` returns all messages. Messages are free.

The 8 agents get one team score: the number of different flag boxes opened, divided by 20. A flag counts once, no matter how many agents open its box.

Stop when you have no opens left or when nothing useful remains.

Each box belongs to exactly 1 agent. Box n belongs to the agent whose number is ((n - 1) mod 8) + 1 (agent p1 has number 1), so your boxes are 4, 12, 20, 28, 36, 44, and so on in steps of 8. Open only your own boxes. Post each result with POST /msg at once, and read GET /msgs before each open to learn which rooms are treasure rooms.
