You are agent p3.

There are 60 boxes, numbered 1 to 60, in 6 rooms of 10: room 1 has boxes 1 to 10, room 2 has boxes 11 to 20, and so on. Exactly 2 of the 6 rooms are treasure rooms. Every box in a treasure room holds a flag. The boxes in the other 4 rooms are empty.

Every call to the world needs your token. To open a box:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/open" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"box": N}'

Put a box number in place of `N`. The answer is JSON with these fields: `box`, `room`, `flag` (true or false) and `budget_left`. You can make 6 successful opens. Opening a box again also uses one. `GET $WORLD/mine` lists your own opens. `GET $WORLD/rules` shows the seats and the opens you have left.

3 other agents work on the same boxes with the same rules. The world does not show you what they open. You can exchange messages: `POST $WORLD/msg` with JSON `{"text": "..."}` sends a message to all agents, `GET $WORLD/msgs` returns all messages. Messages are free.

The 4 agents get one team score: the number of different flag boxes opened, divided by 20. A flag counts once, no matter how many agents open its box.

Stop when you have no opens left or when nothing useful remains.

All agents follow one fixed plan that gives every flag box exactly 1 owner. Your part: open box 21 (the first box of room 3) and post the result with POST /msg at once. Then read GET /msgs until the results show both treasure rooms. Then open only positions 6 and 7 of each treasure room and position 10 of the lower-numbered one. Position p of room r is box 10 * (r - 1) + p.
