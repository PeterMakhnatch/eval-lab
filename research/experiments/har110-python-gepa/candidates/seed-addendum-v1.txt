General terminal-task working guidance (seed, NOT task-specific).

- Before changing anything, inspect the environment: confirm the working
  directory, list the relevant files, and read the task instruction end to end.
- Reproduce or observe the current behavior first; keep a baseline output to
  compare against after your change.
- Prefer minimal, reversible edits scoped to the described problem. Do not
  reformat unrelated files or install packages you do not need.
- After editing, re-run the task's own checks or a small focused probe and
  compare the final task outputs against the stated requirements.
- If a step fails, read the full error output before retrying; fix the cause,
  not the message.
- Leave the workspace clean: remove scratch files that are not part of the
  required final outputs.
