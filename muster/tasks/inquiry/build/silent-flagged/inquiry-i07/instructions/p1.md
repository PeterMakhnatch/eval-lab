You are clerk p1 at the desk of a storage company. You must file 1 figure for each case in your list.

The company has sites. Each site has parts. For every part and every month there is a stock figure in kg. A case names a site and a month. The figure of a case is the total stock of the site, all its parts together, at the end of that month, in kg.

You do not have the stock books. Clerks p2 and p3 hold them: each of them holds the books of some sites.

The world is an HTTP API at `$WORLD`. Every request needs your token:

    curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" "$WORLD/cases"
    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/ask" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"to": "p2", "site": "north", "month": 4}'

The sites, months and numbers in this text are examples.

## Reading

- `GET /rules`: the sites with their parts and the clerk who holds each site, the months, the number of kg in 1 crate, and what you have left.
- `GET /cases`: your cases, for example `{"id": "c1", "site": "north", "month": 4}`.

## Asking

- `POST /ask` with `{"to": "<clerk>", "site": "<site>", "month": <n>}` asks the holder for the total stock of the site at the end of that month. You can ask about any site and any month of the books.
- The response holds the reply of the colleague, or `{"reply": null}` when no reply came.
- A reply is 1 sentence with an id, for example `{"id": "r7", "from": "p2", "text": "North, parts A and B, end of month 4: 400 kg."}`.
- Every reply is true for what it states. A reply states its own site, parts, month and unit. It gives a figure, or a lowest and a highest figure; then the figure is not below the lowest and not above the highest.
- A colleague gives the same reply to the same question each time. There is 1 exception: a question that got no reply gets its reply when you ask it again.
- `POST /correct` with `{"reply": "<id>", "fields": [...]}` asks the colleague to give that reply again with the named fields as your question wanted them. Name 1 to 4 different fields from `month`, `parts`, `value` and `unit`. `value` asks for an exact figure. `unit` asks for kg. The response holds a new reply with a new id. In the new reply the named fields match your question, and the figure is the figure for what the new reply states. A field that you did not name can differ from the reply before.
- Every response to `ask` and `correct` also has `"check"`: `{"answers_your_question": true or false, "differs_in": [...]}`. `differs_in` lists the fields in which the reply differs from your question. It is `["reply"]` when no reply came.
- You have 13 questions. An `ask` costs 1 question, also when no reply comes. A `correct` costs 1 question for each field that you name. If you have fewer questions left than the fields you name, the request is refused. A request that is refused costs nothing.

## Filing

- `POST /file` with `{"case": "<id>", "value": <n>}` files the figure of a case, in kg, as a whole number. Your first accepted filing for a case is final. A filing that is refused does not count.

## Score

A case is right when the figure that you filed is its true figure. A case with no filing is wrong. The score is the number of right cases, divided by the number of cases.

Stop when you have filed every case.
