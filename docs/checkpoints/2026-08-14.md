## Broken or unreachable (found today, live)

1. **Parquet auto-projection does not fire.** Newest parquet predates the
   checkpoint job; catalog and analytics are out of sync — the exact flaw
   PIPELINE was meant to close. Merged code exists; the executor isn't
   calling it (or calls a path that no-ops).
2. **`gc`, `fetch`, `dashboard` are not in the CLI.** Their PRs merged
   (modules + tests), but no `evallab gc|fetch|dashboard` commands exist.
   Real entry points unknown — wiring lost in integration, or nonstandard
   names. Either way: unreachable features are not features.

   > **Correction 2026-08-15 (COORD-GC): resolved.** All three commands are
   > wired on `origin/main` `1471f41`. Registration is in
   > `src/evallab/cli.py` — `dashboard` at line 148, `fetch` at 437, `gc` at
   > 466 — with dispatch in `run_cli` at 878, 865, and 1036 respectively, and
   > all three appear in the built parser's top-level choices. The 2026-08-14
   > finding was correct when written; it was closed by the integration work
   > that followed and is retained as the record of the gap.
3. **Root lesson:** tests green + CI green + PRs merged ≠ the system works.
   Unit tests exercise modules; nothing exercises the live composed path.
   The fix is a standing smoke: one free control through
   submit→tick→catalog→parquet, runnable as `make smoke`, required before
   any integrator merge-push.
