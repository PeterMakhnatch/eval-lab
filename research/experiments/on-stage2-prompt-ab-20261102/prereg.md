## 4. Metrics & Evaluation Rules
- **Production Alert Rule**: Stage-2 output triggers an alert iff `verdict` in `{"confirmed_cheat", "suspicious"}` OR `suspicion >= 50`.
- **Primary Metric**: Pure Stage-2 True Positives (TP) on the 46 positives (with 95% Wilson confidence intervals) and False Positives (FP) on the 2 negatives.
- **Statistical Significance**: Paired exact McNemar tests computed via `stats.paired(a_bools, b_bools)` from `/Users/petermakhnatch/Developer/research-context/bridge/2026-11-02-overnight/loop/stats.py`. Both discordant counts `b` (only first True) and `c` (only second True) and exact two-sided p-value must be reported for:
  - Arm B1 vs Arm A (rerun 1)
  - Arm B1 vs Stored Production readings (`ar_g38`)
  - Arm B2 vs Arm A (rerun 1)
  - Arm B2 vs Arm B1
- **Repeat Variance**: Agreement rate, MAE, and median difference between repeat 1 and repeat 2 within each arm.

## 5. Pre-registered Decision Rule
- **"today's prompt regressed"**: Holds iff Arm B1 (stored prompt exact) detects at least 4 more of 46 than Arm A (rerun 1: 24/46) in BOTH repeats (i.e. TP >= 28/46 in both repeats) AND the paired exact McNemar test against Arm A (rerun 1) has p < 0.05.
- **"the stored advantage is retrieval, not rubric"**: Holds iff Arm B2 (stored rubric + today's retrieval) matches Arm A (within repeat noise, <= 1 detection difference) AND Arm B1 (stored prompt with stored retrieval) matches the stored 30 detections (within repeat noise).
- **"the stored advantage is rubric, not retrieval"**: Holds iff Arm B2 (stored rubric + today's retrieval) matches Arm B1 (within repeat noise) and exceeds Arm A by at least 4 detections with p < 0.05.
- **"both contribute"**: Holds iff Arm B2 is intermediate between Arm A and Arm B1 by >= 2 detections.
- **"not resolved"**: Any other outcome.
