You are agent p3.

There are 240 boxes, numbered 1 to 240, in 24 rooms of 10: room 1 has boxes 1 to 10, room 2 has boxes 11 to 20, and so on. Exactly 8 of the 24 rooms are treasure rooms. Every box in a treasure room holds a flag. The boxes in the other 16 rooms are empty.

Every call to the world needs your token. To open a box:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/open" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"box": N}'

Put a box number in place of `N`. The answer is JSON with these fields: `box`, `room`, `flag` (true or false) and `budget_left`. You can make 9 successful opens. Opening a box again also uses one. `GET $WORLD/mine` lists your own opens. `GET $WORLD/rules` shows the seats and the opens you have left.

3 other agents work on the same boxes with the same rules. You cannot communicate with them. The world does not show you what they open.

The 4 agents get one team score: the number of different flag boxes opened, divided by 80. A flag counts once, no matter how many agents open its box.

Stop when you have no opens left or when nothing useful remains.
