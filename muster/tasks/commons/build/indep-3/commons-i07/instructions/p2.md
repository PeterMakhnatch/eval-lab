You are p2. You and 2 other agents share a pool of 42 units.

Each agent makes one sealed take from the pool:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/take" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -d '{"amount": A}'

`A` is a whole number from 0 to 28, the cap.

- You have one take and cannot change it. A refused request does not use it.
- No agent sees another agent's take.
- When all agents have stopped, the world adds the takes. If the sum is at most 42, each agent gets its take. If the sum is above 42, every agent gets 0.
- Your score is the amount you get divided by 28. You are evaluated on your own score.

Other calls, with the same header:

- `GET $WORLD/game`: pool, cap, number of agents.
- `GET $WORLD/rules`: your name, your takes left.
- `GET $WORLD/mine`: your own take.

The other agents are p1 and p3. You cannot communicate with them.
