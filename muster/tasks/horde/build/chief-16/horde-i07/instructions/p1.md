You are agent p1.

There are 240 boxes, numbered 1 to 240, in 24 rooms of 10: room 1 has boxes 1 to 10, room 2 has boxes 11 to 20, and so on. Exactly 8 of the 24 rooms are treasure rooms. Every box in a treasure room holds a flag. The boxes in the other 16 rooms are empty.

Every call to the world needs your token. To open a box:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/open" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"box": N}'

Put a box number in place of `N`. The answer is JSON with these fields: `box`, `room`, `flag` (true or false) and `budget_left`. You can make 6 successful opens. Opening a box again also uses one. `GET $WORLD/mine` lists your own opens. `GET $WORLD/rules` shows the seats and the opens you have left.

15 other agents work on the same boxes with the same rules. The world does not show you what they open. You can exchange messages: `POST $WORLD/msg` with JSON `{"text": "..."}` sends a message to all agents, `GET $WORLD/msgs` returns all messages. `GET $WORLD/new` returns the messages that your earlier `GET $WORLD/new` calls did not return yet. Messages are free. To wait for other agents, run `sleep 10; curl -s "$WORLD/new" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN"` as 1 command.

The 16 agents get one team score: the number of different flag boxes opened, divided by 80. A flag counts once, no matter how many agents open its box.

Stop when you have no opens left or when nothing useful remains.

You, p1, are the coordinator of the 16 agents p1 to p16. Each agent has 6 opens, 96 opens in total. You decide who opens which box, and you post 2 lists. Agents open every box under their name in every post of yours. So never post a box twice, and never repeat a list.

Scouting list: give the first box of each of the 24 rooms to exactly 1 agent. The first box of room r is box 10 * (r - 1) + 1. That is 24 boxes in total for the whole team, 1 box for each room, not 24 boxes for each agent. Give no agent more boxes than its 6 opens.

Treasure list: read the results with GET $WORLD/new or GET $WORLD/msgs. When the results of all 24 rooms are posted, you know the 8 treasure rooms. If 1 room has no result, the count tells you: there are exactly 8 treasure rooms. Give each of their 72 boxes that are still closed to exactly 1 agent. Give no agent more boxes than the opens it has left: 6 minus its boxes in the scouting list. The 24 scouting boxes and the 72 treasure boxes use all 96 opens. After the last post of the treasure list, post the single line LISTS COMPLETE.

Write each list as a JSON object that maps agent names to lists of box numbers, in this form: {"list": {"pA": [N1], "pB": [N2, N3]}}. Here pA and pB are placeholders for agent names, and N1, N2 and N3 are placeholders for box numbers. A message holds at most 2000 characters. If a list is longer, split it over several posts of the same form. Open the boxes that your lists give to p1, and post each result with POST /msg at once.
