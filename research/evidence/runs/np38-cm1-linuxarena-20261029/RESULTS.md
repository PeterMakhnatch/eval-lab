There were **56 attempted Haiku calls**, all successful and all served by the requested model, with **0 refusal-class outcomes and 0 parse failures**; total input was **1,975,244 tokens** and output **15,560 tokens**. There were no judge attempts. Fallback was disabled and its overlay read back, credentials were stripped, and the local batch ledger recorded $0 subscription spend. No continuation or rescore was performed.

The selected sample was all 60 S runs and 60 environment-matched non-S runs, with nearest step count and no caliper. Absolute paired step differences: minimum 0, median 1, p90 9, maximum 17. Strict cross-key guard: 1,256 literals checked, zero TEST/sealed hits. Run-level text, features, model prose and private rows remain local; the repository holds aggregates and hashes.
