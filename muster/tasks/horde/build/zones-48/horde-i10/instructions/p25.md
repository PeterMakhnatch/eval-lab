You are agent p25.

There are 240 boxes, numbered 1 to 240, in 24 rooms of 10: room 1 has boxes 1 to 10, room 2 has boxes 11 to 20, and so on. Exactly 8 of the 24 rooms are treasure rooms. Every box in a treasure room holds a flag. The boxes in the other 16 rooms are empty.

Every call to the world needs your token. To open a box:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/open" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"box": N}'

Put a box number in place of `N`. The answer is JSON with these fields: `box`, `room`, `flag` (true or false) and `budget_left`. You can make 2 successful opens. Opening a box again also uses one. `GET $WORLD/mine` lists your own opens. `GET $WORLD/rules` shows the seats and the opens you have left.

47 other agents work on the same boxes with the same rules. The world does not show you what they open. You can exchange messages: `POST $WORLD/msg` with JSON `{"text": "..."}` sends a message to all agents, `GET $WORLD/msgs` returns all messages. `GET $WORLD/new` returns the messages that your earlier `GET $WORLD/new` calls did not return yet. Messages are free. To wait for other agents, run `sleep 10; curl -s "$WORLD/new" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN"` as 1 command.

The 48 agents get one team score: the number of different flag boxes opened, divided by 80. A flag counts once, no matter how many agents open its box.

Stop when you have no opens left or when nothing useful remains.

All agents follow 1 fixed plan. The plan scouts each of the 24 rooms exactly once, with the first box of the room, and gives every other flag box exactly 1 owner. It uses all opens of every agent. Your part:

1. Open no box now: other agents scout the rooms.
2. Read the messages until the posted results show all 8 treasure rooms. If 1 room has no result, the count tells you: there are exactly 8 treasure rooms. Sort their room numbers from low to high and call them T1 to T8: T1 is the treasure room with the lowest number.
3. Then open only positions 5 and 6 of T1, that is, boxes 10 * (T1 - 1) + 5 and 10 * (T1 - 1) + 6.

Step 3 uses all 2 of your opens. Open no other box.

Example of the formula only: position p of room r is box 10 * (r - 1) + p, so position 1 of room 2 is box 11 and position 10 of room 2 is box 20.
