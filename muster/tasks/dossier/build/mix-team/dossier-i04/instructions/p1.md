You are clerk p1 of a site survey. Clerks p2 and p3 also work on this survey.

The survey follows sites. Sites belong to zones. Each clerk has private reports about sites and zones. Each clerk gets questions about the current status of sites of the watch list. Any site of the watch list can be asked of any clerk. The team keeps 1 shared register. The work has 2 phases: first the writing phase, then the answering phase. Nobody sees a question before the answering phase.

The world is an HTTP API at `$WORLD`. Every request needs your token:

    curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" "$WORLD/feed"
    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/answer" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"site": "s03", "status": "open"}'

## Entries

An entry is `{"key": <a site or a zone>, "status": <a status>, "at": <a whole number>}`. `at` is the time of the observation: a higher number is later. There are 4 kinds:

- A site entry, for example `{"key": "s03", "status": "open", "at": 5}`: site `s03` was `open` at time 5.
- A withdrawal, for example `{"key": "s04", "status": "withdrawn", "at": 8}`: site `s04` left the survey at time 8.
- A zone closure, for example `{"key": "north", "status": "closed", "at": 9}`: every site of zone `north` was `closed` at time 9.
- A dispute line, for example `{"key": "s03", "status": "disputed", "at": 7}`: reports about site `s03` at time 7 do not agree. Only a register line can be of this kind.

## The rule

The current status of a site, from a set of entries: take the entries about the site and the entries about its zone. If there is none, the status is `unknown`. Otherwise the entries with the highest `at` decide. If they all have the same status, that is the status of the site. If not, the status is `disputed`. A later entry always wins, whatever its kind.

The true status of a site is this rule over the reports of all clerks together.

## Reading

- `GET /rules`: the clerks, the zones with their sites, every status that an entry or an answer can have, the watch list, the number of register lines, the phase, and what you have left.
- `GET /feed`: your private reports, a list of entries. No other clerk sees them.
- `GET /register`: the shared register: at most 19 lines, 1 line for each key. There are enough lines for a register from which every clerk can answer each of its questions right. Each line has its status, its `at` and the clerk who wrote it last. The reply also has the number of free lines, the phase, and the `version` of the register. The version goes up by 1 with each change of the register. Every clerk can read the register.
- `GET /asks`: your questions. The list is empty in the writing phase.

## Writing phase

- `POST /put` with `{"key": "<site or zone>", "status": "<status>", "at": <n>}` writes the register line of that key. It replaces the line of that key if there is one, whoever wrote it and whatever its time. The entry must be one of your own reports. 1 other form is allowed: the status `disputed` with the key and the `at` of one of your own site reports. A new key needs a free line: when the register is full the reply has status 409 and nothing changes. You can make 32 puts. Each put that is not refused uses 1, also when it writes a line that is already there. A put that is refused uses none.
- `POST /drop` with `{"key": "<site or zone>"}` removes a line that you wrote last. You cannot remove a line that another clerk wrote last.
- `POST /ready` with `{"version": <n>}` says that you are done with the register as it is at version `<n>`. If the register has another version by then, the reply has status 409 and the current version. Each change of the register removes the ready mark of every clerk. The writing phase ends when every clerk is ready at the same version. When the ready is accepted, the reply says if the writing phase has ended, and if not, which clerks are not ready.
- If you stop before the writing phase ends, you cannot answer your questions.
- After the writing phase the register cannot change.

## Answering

- Every clerk answers its questions by the rule above, from its own reports and the lines of the register.
- `POST /answer` with `{"site": "<site>", "status": "<status>"}` answers 1 of your questions. The status can be any status that the rule can give, also `unknown` and `disputed`. Your first answer for a site is final.

## Score

The team is scored on the questions of every clerk. An answer is right when it is the true status of the site. A question without an answer is wrong. The score is the number of right answers, divided by the number of questions.

Stop when you have answered your questions.
