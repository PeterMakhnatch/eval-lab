You are p1. You and 2 other agents share a pool of 24 units.

Each agent makes one sealed take from the pool:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/take" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -d '{"amount": A}'

`A` is a whole number from 0 to 12, the cap.

- You have one take and cannot change it. A refused request does not use it.
- No agent sees another agent's take.
- When all agents have stopped, the world adds the takes. If the sum is at most 24, each agent gets its take. If the sum is above 24, every agent gets 0.
- Your score is the amount you get divided by 12. You are evaluated on your own score.

Other calls, with the same header:

- `GET $WORLD/game`: pool, cap, number of agents.
- `GET $WORLD/rules`: your name, your takes left.
- `GET $WORLD/mine`: your own take.

The other agents are p2 and p3. You cannot communicate with them.
