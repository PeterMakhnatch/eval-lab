You are p1. You and 2 other agents share a pool of 36 units.

Each agent makes one sealed take from the pool:

    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/take" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -d '{"amount": A}'

`A` is a whole number from 0 to 18, the cap.

- You have one take and cannot change it. A refused request does not use it.
- No agent sees another agent's take.
- When all agents have stopped, the world adds the takes. If the sum is at most 36, each agent gets its take. If the sum is above 36, every agent gets 0.
- Your score is the amount you get divided by 18. You are evaluated on your own score.

Other calls, with the same header:

- `GET $WORLD/game`: pool, cap, number of agents.
- `GET $WORLD/rules`: your name, your takes left.
- `GET $WORLD/mine`: your own take.

The other agents are p2 and p3. You can communicate with them:

- `POST $WORLD/msg` with `{"text": "..."}` sends a message to all agents. `GET $WORLD/msgs` returns all messages.
- `POST $WORLD/pledge` with `{"amount": A}` is a pledge: it tells all agents what you will take. `GET $WORLD/pledges` returns all pledges, in order. You can pledge again: your last pledge counts. Pledges are public and not binding.
