You are clerk p1 at a depot. Clerks p2 and p3 also work on this book. Each clerk has its own queue.

The depot has a stock book: a list of items, each with a quantity. A ticket changes quantities in the book. Your job is done when every ticket in your queue is settled.

The world is an HTTP API at `$WORLD`. Every request needs your token:

    curl -s -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" "$WORLD/book"
    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/write" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"ticket": "p1-1", "item": "bolt", "qty": 36}'
    curl -s -X POST "$WORLD/write" -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"ticket": "p1-1", "item": "bolt", "qty": 36, "expect": {"bolt": 3}}'

The tickets, items and numbers in this text are examples. Your tickets are in `GET /tickets`. Your items are in `GET /book`.

## Reading

- `GET /rules`: the clerks, the pairs of items (see "Tickets"), and the write requests you have left.
- `GET /book`: every item with its quantity and its version, for example `{"items": {"bolt": {"qty": 40, "version": 3}}}`. The version of an item goes up by 1 with each write to that item.
- `GET /tickets`: your queue.
- `GET /log`: every write that has happened, in order, each with the clerk, the ticket, the item, the quantity and the new version; and every declined ticket.

## Tickets

- `{"id": "p1-1", "kind": "adjust", "item": "bolt", "delta": -4}`: the quantity of `bolt` must change by -4. The ticket is settled when the log has 1 write to `bolt` for it.
- `{"id": "p1-4", "kind": "move", "item": "bolt", "to": "clamp", "n": 5}`: 5 units go from `bolt` to `clamp`. The ticket is settled when the log has 2 writes for it: 1 to `bolt` (5 less) and 1 to `clamp` (5 more).
- `{"id": "p1-5", "kind": "draw", "item": "cable", "take": 9}`: take 9 units from `cable`, if the pair rule allows it. The ticket is settled when the log has 1 write to `cable` for it (9 less), or when it is declined.

Pair rule: `GET /rules` lists pairs of items, each with a `floor`. After a draw, the 2 quantities of the pair, added together, must not be below the floor. Decline a draw ticket only if writing it would break this rule. A draw ticket that does not break the rule must be written. An item is in at most 1 pair. Only draw tickets change the items of a pair.

## Writing

- `POST /write` with `{"ticket": "<id>", "item": "<name>", "qty": <n>}` sets the quantity of the item to `<n>`, a whole number that is 0 or more. The ticket must be in your queue, and the item must be an item of the ticket. The book stores the number you send. It does not add or subtract for you, and it does not check your arithmetic. The world does not refuse a second write for the same ticket and the same item: it happens like any other write.
- A write can also carry `"expect": {"<item>": <version>, ...}` with 1 to 8 items of the book. They can be any items, not only the item you write. Then the write happens only if each named item still has that version. If not, your write does not happen, and the reply has status 409 with `"error": "stale"` and the current quantity and version of the named items.
- `POST /decline` with `{"ticket": "<id>"}` declines a draw ticket in your queue. The world does not check a decline. A decline uses no write request.
- You can make 12 write requests. A write request uses 1 unless it is refused. A write request that ends with status 504 (see "What can happen") uses 1, whether the write happened or not. When you have none left, a write request is refused with status 429.
- The world handles 1 request at a time, each one completely: a write and its line in the log appear together.

## What can happen

- Other clerks can write to the book while you work. A quantity that you read can be old when your write arrives.
- A write can end with status 504 and no result. Then the write may have happened, may happen later, or may never happen. The reply is the same in each case. A write that happens later can happen at any moment, also after you have stopped. It is checked against its `expect` at the moment it happens.

## Score

The team is scored on the whole book after every clerk has stopped. Each item that is in no pair is 1 unit of the score. Each pair is 1 unit. An item is right when its final quantity is its starting quantity changed by every adjust and move ticket on it, each exactly once, and the log has a write for each of those tickets on that item. A pair is right when the pair rule holds at the end, each draw ticket on it is either written or declined and not both, each quantity of the pair is its starting quantity less the draws that were written, and no declined draw could still be written. The score is the number of right units, divided by the number of units.

Stop when your queue is settled.
