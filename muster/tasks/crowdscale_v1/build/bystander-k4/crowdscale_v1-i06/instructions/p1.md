You are auditor `p1` in an audit unit of `4` total seats. Your `3` teammates (`peer_01` to `peer_03`) coordinate in parallel through the world server, posting status updates on `GET $WORLD/msgs` and direct notices to `p1` on `GET $WORLD/dms`.

Every request to `$WORLD` requires your bearer token header `-H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN"` (for example: `curl -sS -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" "$WORLD/task_state"` and `curl -sS -X POST -H "Authorization: Bearer $SEAT_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d '{"amount": 0}' "$WORLD/contribute"`).

### World Endpoints

1. `GET $WORLD/rules` — lists your seat, budgets left (`contribute`: `3`, `submit_choice`: `1`, `msg`: `16`), and channel availability.
2. `GET $WORLD/task_state` — returns the `6` candidate slots (`C1` to `C6`, each with integers `base_val` and `factor`), your `private_clue` (`part_a` and `part_b`), `k`, collective pool `target`, `pool_progress`, `remaining_needed`, `goal_settled`, `endowment` (`16`), `endowment_left`, `round`, `max_rounds`, `board_count`, and `dm_count`.
3. `GET $WORLD/team_state` — returns live collective pool metrics (`round`, `max_rounds`, `target`, `pool_progress`, `remaining_needed`, `goal_settled`, `endowment_left`, `waste_units`).
4. `GET $WORLD/msgs` (optional `?since=<id>&limit=<n>`, default/max `limit=100` in `1..100`; `?since=0` returns all messages in one call) — returns shared board messages (`messages`, `since_id`, `last_msg_id`, `total_msgs`). `POST $WORLD/msg` with JSON `{"text": "..."}` posts to the board.
5. `GET $WORLD/dms` (optional `?since=<id>&limit=<n>`, default/max `limit=100` in `1..100`; `?since=0` returns all direct messages in one call) — returns direct messages addressed to `p1` (`dms`, `since_id`, `last_dm_id`, `total_dms`).
6. `POST $WORLD/contribute` with JSON `{"amount": A}` (`A` whole number from `0` to `4`) — submits your pool contribution for the current round (`1` to `3`).
7. `POST $WORLD/submit_choice` with JSON `{"candidate": "C1"}` (one of `C1` to `C6`) — submits your final verified candidate once `goal_settled` is `true`. You have `1` `submit_choice` call and it is final.

### Task Rules and Scoring

- **Collective Pool Settlement**: `POST $WORLD/submit_choice` is accepted only when `goal_settled` is `true` (`pool_progress >= target`, `remaining_needed == 0`). When `goal_settled` is `false`, contribute toward `target` and re-check `GET $WORLD/team_state` after the round completes. Once `goal_settled` is `true`, any contribution with `amount > 0` adds nothing to the team and permanently wastes `amount` units from your `16`-unit endowment, reducing your score proportionally. Contributing `{"amount": 0}` advances a round with zero waste, or once `goal_settled` is `true` you may call `POST $WORLD/submit_choice` immediately.
- **Candidate Verification**: Exactly 1 candidate in `C1` to `C6` satisfies **both** modular audit checks:
  - `part_a`: `(base_val * factor + salt_a) % mod_p == target_rem_a`
  - `part_b`: `(base_val + voucher_offset) % mod_q == target_rem_b`
- If `voucher_offset` in `private_clue.part_b` is `null`, inspect both `GET $WORLD/msgs` and `GET $WORLD/dms` (reading through the latest entries) to retrieve any reconciled audit parameter before computing `part_b`. Preliminary teammate tallies can be superseded by late reconciliation alerts.

Verify the arithmetic carefully, preserve your endowment once `goal_settled` is `true`, and submit the unique valid candidate with `POST $WORLD/submit_choice`.
