# R2-REVIEW-LOAD: Judge Station Review Load Simulation & Throttling Policy Evaluation

## Overview

This experiment evaluates the expected human review load generated by the opt-in AutoReview live loop (HAR-B1-HOOK / B2-REPLAY-PROOF) and compares three throttling policies against gold benchmark labels from TraceMonitor-Bench DEV v1 (`BENCH_ROOT`).

When the live loop is unthrottled, deterministic tripwires (`l0_gate`), cascade escalations (`cascade_stage1`), and target surface writes (`target_surface`) trigger on **60.89%** of admitted DEV runs. At observed production factory arrival rates (541.5 runs/day), the loop creates **~330 cards/day**, requiring **11.0 to 16.5 hours of daily judging time** from a single reviewer. At 10x scale (5,415 runs/day), it generates **~3,300 cards/day** (110–165 judge-hours/day).

Throttling is mandatory before enabling live AutoReview. We evaluate three throttling policies across daily card budgets $K \in \{10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 75, 100\}$ to maximize expected true positives surfaced per judge-hour while capping human review to $\le 2$ hours/day.

---

## Key Findings

1. **Observed Arrival Rates**:
   - Production factory cycle log span: 57.68 hours (2.404 days), processing 1,303 admitted DEV runs across 13,458 cycles.
   - Steady daily arrival rate: **541.5 runs/day** (22.6 runs/hour).
   - Peak burst replay throughput: **20.96 runs/min** (1,257.6 runs/hour).
   - 10x scale scenario: **5,414.6 runs/day**.

2. **Empirical Trigger Rates per 100 Runs (DEV Cohort, n=404)**:
   - `terminal_wrench` (n=304): L0 gate 41.12% [35.7%, 46.8%], Cascade Stage 1 16.12% [12.4%, 20.7%], Target Surface 0.00% [0.0%, 1.2%]. Unique triggered runs: **48.03%** [42.4%, 53.7%].
   - `goodfire_deepswe` (n=100): L0 gate 38.00% [29.1%, 47.8%], Cascade Stage 1 98.00% [93.0%, 99.5%], Target Surface 98.00% [93.0%, 99.5%]. Unique triggered runs: **100.00%** [96.3%, 100.0%].
   - **Pooled Admitted Cohort (n=404)**:
     - `l0_gate`: **40.35%** (95% Wilson: 35.68% – 45.20%)
     - `cascade_stage1`: **36.39%** (95% Wilson: 31.84% – 41.19%)
     - `target_surface`: **24.26%** (95% Wilson: 20.33% – 28.67%)
     - **Unique runs generating cards**: **60.89%** (95% Wilson: 56.05% – 65.53%)

3. **Policy Comparison (TraceMonitor-Bench DEV v1 Report Split, 387 cases, 188 positives)**:
   - **Policy 1: Top-K Priority per Day**:
     - At $K=30$ cards/day: **30 / 30 True Positives** (**100.0% precision**, 95% Wilson: [88.6%, 100.0%]), surfacing **24.0 TP/judge-hour** at 2.5 min/card (30.0 at 2m, 20.0 at 3m).
     - Daily judge commitment: **1.25 hours/day** (1.0h at 2m, 1.5h at 3m).
     - Statistically significantly outperforms uniform random sampling ($p = 0.0336$ via exact McNemar).
   - **Policy 2: Per-Family Caps**:
     - At $K=30$ cards/day: **21 / 30 True Positives** (**70.0% precision**, 95% Wilson: [52.1%, 83.3%]), surfacing **16.8 TP/judge-hour**. Reserving slots for low-prevalence families dilutes precision.
   - **Policy 3: Uncertainty Sampling + Random Controls (80/20)**:
     - At $K=30$ cards/day: **29 / 30 True Positives** (**96.7% precision**, 95% Wilson: [83.3%, 99.4%]), surfacing **23.2 TP/judge-hour**. Provides sentinel coverage of Priority 0 false negatives with modest efficiency loss.
   - **Baseline: Uniform Random Sampling**:
     - At $K=30$ cards/day: **16 / 30 True Positives** (**53.3% precision**, 95% Wilson: [36.1%, 69.8%]), surfacing **12.8 TP/judge-hour**.

4. **Recommendation**:
   - **Default Daily Card Budget**: **30 cards/day** (safe operating band: **20 to 40 cards/day**).
   - **Default Throttling Policy**: **Policy 1 (Top-K Priority per Day)**.
   - **Operator Rationale**: Maximizes true positive discovery per unit of human effort (24.0 TP/hr at 2.5 min/card) while bounding daily judge commitment to 1.0–1.5 hours/day.

5. **Pending 69-Verdict Human Calibration**:
   - 69 unjudged cards currently wait in Judge Station queues (`guide-examples`: 4, `adjudication-packet`: 18, `goodfire-action-gold`: 23, `goodfire-census`: 24).
   - Once completed by a human reviewer, this calibration will provide empirical measurement of human judging speed $\tau_h$ and human-vs-label discordance $\delta_h$.
   - A 10% human-label discordance shifts effective yield from 24.0 to 21.6 TP/hr, while preserving the strict dominance of Policy 1.
